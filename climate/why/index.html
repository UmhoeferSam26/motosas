Why it works

One room. One system. Every season.

All-in-one climate systems use heat-pump technology to move heat in or out of a room, providing both heating and cooling without a conventional outdoor condenser.

How the technology works

Everything is contained inside one wall-mounted unit.

The sealed refrigeration circuit, compressor and fans sit within the indoor unit. Two discreet openings through the external wall allow the system to exchange air and heat with the outside, while condensate is managed through the installation.

In cooling mode, the system removes heat from the room and releases it outside. In heating mode, the cycle reverses, drawing available heat from outside air and transferring it indoors.
